# Colour

## Three colours, and what each is for

The default is **two subject colours plus one background**. Not two, not five.

- **Base.** The dominant mass. It is what the mark is "the blue one" for.
- **Second.** One large continuous region — a face mask, a belly, a shell, a
  visor — and the small facial marks reuse it. Never scattered into patches.
- **Background.** A flat field. It belongs to the background only, and never
  appears as a painted region inside the subject.

Use more only when asked. For a two-colour mark, the eyes and mouth become
negative-space cutouts that reveal the background, rather than a third colour.

## Choosing them

Judge colour in OKLCH, not in HSL saturation, which lies about how vivid a
colour looks at a given lightness.

| Surface                       | Lightness   | Chroma      |
| ----------------------------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| Chromatic mid background      | `0.45–0.75` | `0.08–0.16` |
| Dark chromatic background     | `0.18–0.35` | `0.05–0.14` |
| Cream or parchment background | `0.92–0.98` | `0.01–0.06` |

Below about `0.05` chroma a background reads grey and lifeless; above about
`0.20` it reads neon. Both are worth avoiding unless asked for.

Prefer a warm off-white over pure white and a charcoal or deep navy over pure
black — pure ends of the scale look like an absence of a decision. Prefer
softened hues: terracotta, dusty plum, sage, denim, ochre.

## Contrast is a floor, not a preference

- Silhouette against background: at least **3:1**.
- Small facial marks against whatever is under them: at least **4.5:1**.

If a requested palette misses these, keep the requested background and move the
subject colours. The background is usually the part somebody chose on purpose.

## Depth, if any

Flat is a finished answer. If the mark is modelled at all, it is modelled by
**one continuous gradient per large region**, all sharing one light direction
from the upper left. Each transition spans at least half the width of the form
it is on.

Keep the drift inside the colour's own family: hue within about 3°, chroma
within `0.015`, lightness within `0.08` peak to peak. The modelling should be
visible at full size and effectively gone at 32 × 32, leaving flat colour.

Never model depth as a _shape_: no highlight blob with its own outline, no
banded steps, no cel-shaded regions, no second contour inside the first, no
contact-shadow blob. A highlight with an edge is another shape competing with
the silhouette.

Keep the background flat. No vignette, no directional wash.

## Reject the result when

- The colour count is not the one asked for.
- The second colour is scattered rather than one region.
- The background colour appears as a painted region inside the subject.
- Shading introduced a hue that is not in the palette.
- A gradient banded, changed direction between adjacent parts, or produced a
  small glossy hotspot.
- The background is neon, or so grey it reads as dead.
- Either contrast floor is missed.
- At 32 × 32 the modelling still reads as an extra colour region.
